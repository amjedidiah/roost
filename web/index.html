roost

Never miss what your documents say is due.

Add your bills, bookings and letters. roost finds every deadline written in them and emails you before each one.

  1. Add your documents. Word or text files, or try the sample ones.
  2. roost finds the dates. Only what is written in your files, never guessed.
  3. Get a reminder. A short email the week something is due.

    roost runs on a Fly.io Sprite. It reads your files from Box, remembers them in Pinecone and sends email through AgentMail, all through Sprites connectors, so the server holds no passwords or keys. Dates come from fixed rules over your own words, not an AI chatbot.
