# Weekend Sourdough

*My working version after about a dozen bakes. Makes one medium loaf.*

## Ingredients

- 100 g active starter (fed 4–6 hours before, bubbly and doubled)
- 375 g water, lukewarm
- 450 g strong white bread flour
- 50 g wholemeal flour
- 10 g salt

## Method

1. **Mix:** stir the starter into 350 g of the water, add both flours and mix until no dry bits
   remain. Rest 45 minutes (autolyse).
2. **Salt:** add the salt and the remaining 25 g water, and squeeze it through the dough.
3. **Bulk ferment:** 4–5 hours at about 24°C. Do 4 sets of stretch-and-folds, 30 minutes apart, in
   the first 2 hours. It's ready when the dough has grown about 50% and jiggles.
4. **Shape:** pre-shape into a round, rest 20 minutes, then shape tight and put it seam-up in a
   floured banneton.
5. **Cold proof:** overnight in the fridge, 12–16 hours.
6. **Bake:** preheat the oven and Dutch oven to 250°C for 45 minutes. Score, bake 20 minutes with
   the lid on, then 22–25 minutes lid off at 230°C until deep brown.
7. **Cool** at least 1 hour before cutting.

## Notes from past bakes

- In winter the kitchen is colder, so bulk takes closer to 6 hours. Use the oven with only the
  light on.
- Too much wholemeal (over 15%) made the crumb dense.
- Rice flour in the banneton stops sticking far better than wheat flour.
